01 Bring your video

Make clips = one click: download → AI picks best moments → captioned 9:16 clips. Uses the Clips / length / layout / caption style below.
Only a part of the video? optional, downloads just that part

Leave empty to use the whole video. Up to 2 hours at once. Short videos download fast; a part cut from a long video is fetched at about real-time speed (a 30-minute part takes roughly 15 to 20 minutes), so pick only what you need.

YouTube blocked or country-restricted? browser login, GitHub proxies & auto-rotation

For bot checks, borrow your browser login. For region-locked videos ("available in India" etc.), enable GitHub proxy lists + auto-rotation (only YouTube fetches use it, without sending your login cookies to public proxies).

🤖 Long stream? Let a free chatbot pick the moments NEW

A chatbot given a 9-hour transcript only really reads the start. So: split the transcript into small parts, paste each part into any free chatbot (Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude…), paste its answers back here, and NeonClipping downloads only those sections and makes the clips. Needs a YouTube video with captions. Nothing is sent anywhere by NeonClipping.
